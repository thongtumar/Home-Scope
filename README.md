This repository contains a single-page interactive web application developed as an **experimental stimulus** for Master's thesis research in Marketing at the University of Economics Ho Chi Minh City (UEH). 

The platform simulates a neutral proptech portal ("HomeScope") presenting a hypothetical green residential project ("An Phú Green Residence") to investigate consumer behavioral responses.

---

## 🎯 Research Context & Objectives

The primary objective of this stimulus is not commercial conversion or sales promotion, but rather to methodologically isolate and measure the effects of **AI-Supported Data Transparency (AIDT)** on structural constructs:
1. **AIDT (AI-Supported Data Transparency):** Evaluating algorithmic information synthesis, processing logic, and source provenance.
2. **GTR (Green Trust):** Consumer confidence in the environmental claims of the project.
3. **PU (Perceived Usefulness):** Evaluation of the AI assistant's effectiveness in decision-making support.
4. **PGW (Perceived Greenwashing):** Consumer skepticism triggered by selective disclosure versus missing empirical data.
5. **PIN (Purchase Intention):** Behavioral intention toward the sustainable property offering.

---

## 🔬 Methodological Design Principles (Internal Validity Control)

To prevent experimental bias, confounding, and demand characteristics, the interface strictly adheres to the following rules:
* **Confound Control:** Fixed property pricing and specific location anchors are intentionally neutralized to avoid biasing willingness-to-pay (WTP) or location preferences.
* **Neutral Framing:** The AI system is described functionally (summarizing, cross-referencing, mapping data sources) without using subjective or leading adjectives such as *"transparent," "objective," "verified,"* or *"100% accurate."*
* **Balanced Disclosure:** Combines positive verbal green claims (e.g., LED lighting, Low-E glass, water-saving fixtures) with explicitly highlighted information gaps (e.g., lack of annual utility savings estimates or independent post-occupancy audit data) to accurately test Perceived Greenwashing (PGW).
* **Zero Sales Pressure:** Completely devoid of countdown timers, scarcity tactics ("only X units left"), pop-up sales, testimonials, or aggressive CTAs ("Buy Now").

## 🛠️ Technical Stack

* **Core Framework:** React (Single-file component architecture)
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Clean, neutral proptech aesthetic with off-white backgrounds, charcoal typography, and subtle green accents)
* **Icons:** Lucide React (Neutral system iconography)
* **Deployment Readiness:** Fully responsive across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile devices (Optimized primarily for desktop screen-based survey environments like Qualtrics or Google Forms).
